Love & Chaos — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Who we are and what this covers

Love & Chaos is a single-player, offline combination game published by EDW Ventures LLC for Android devices through the Google Play Store. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. It covers the Love & Chaos Android app (package com.edwventures.loveandchaos). It does not cover the Google Play Store itself; your use of Google Play is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The short version

  • We do not collect your personal data. No name, no email, no account, no advertising ID, no location — nothing.
  • The game runs fully offline. You can play the entire free game with no network connection and no sign-in.
  • The only time the app uses the internet is to handle your one-time purchase through Google Play — to unlock, verify, or restore the full game. Nothing about how you play is sent to us.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app saves is kept in the app's own private storage on your device, as ordinary game data. None of it identifies you. It includes:

  • Your game progress — which discoveries and recipes you have found (stored as internal item codes, not personal information).
  • Your settings — music, sound-effects, haptics, and reduced-motion toggles, and whether you have finished the tutorial.
  • Game screen state — your recently used and pinned cards, and combinations you have already tried that did not work (so the game does not keep re-suggesting them).
  • A full-game unlock marker — a simple note of whether you have unlocked the full game. Google Play is the real record of your purchase; this on-device marker only lets the app show the right state quickly. It holds no payment details, no order number, and no account information.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it.

A note on Android backup: because this data lives in the app's private storage, if you have Android's device backup turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your own backup. That backup belongs to you and your Google account, is controlled by you, and is not accessible to us.

What we collect and share

Nothing personal. To be specific:

  • No accounts and no sign-in. The game has no login of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, measurement, attribution, or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. We have nothing to sell or share, because we do not collect anything.

The app does not transmit your gameplay, your settings, or any identifier to us or to any analytics or advertising service.

If the app ever crashes or stops responding, Google Play may show us aggregate, non-identifying stability information through Google Play's built-in "Android vitals." That is a standard Google Play Console feature, not software we added to the app, and it does not involve any data collection by the app.

Permissions and why

  • The app requests internet access for one reason only: to handle your purchase through Google Play's billing system — so Google Play can unlock, verify, acknowledge, and restore the full game. All gameplay stays fully offline; the app sends no gameplay data and no personal data over the network.
  • The app requests no other sensitive permissions. It has no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, or device identifiers. (It contains only a standard internal Android permission, added automatically by the Android support libraries, that lets the app's own components talk to one another; it grants no access to anything on your device.)

We keep this policy matched to the software actually shipped in the app. The final version of this policy is confirmed against the exact set of libraries and permissions in the released build at the time of release.

Purchases

Love & Chaos is free to download and includes a free set of discoveries you can play offline. There is one optional, one-time purchase that permanently unlocks the full game. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no "energy," no consumable purchases, and no paid hints.

All purchases are handled entirely by Google Play's billing system. We never receive or store your payment details — not your card number, not your billing address, nothing. Google processes the payment and tells the app only whether the full game is unlocked. The price you see is the one Google Play displays for your country and currency.

If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the full game re-locks, but the discoveries you have already found are kept and are restored if your access returns. Your progress is never erased by a purchase, a refund, or by reinstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Love & Chaos is a comedy about romance and soap-opera-style drama. Its humor refers to adult relationship situations and includes mild, non-graphic references to things such as alcohol; the app's Google Play content rating reflects this. It is written for a general teen-and-adult audience and is not directed to young children.

Whatever your age, the app collects no personal information from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not build profiles, show ads, or track users. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

  • We hold no copy of your data on any server — we do not operate one.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device. If you have Android backup turned on, a copy may remain in your own Google account backup until you delete it there or it expires; that copy is yours and is not accessible to us.
  • Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's policies. You can review that, or ask Google to delete it, through Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. Because the policy is tied to the software actually shipped, we update it in step with any change to the app's libraries, permissions, or data flows — for example, if a future version were ever to add analytics, that change and this policy would ship together.
